English keyboard language model (en.hextm): derived from Google Books Ngram
Viewer, version 3 (2020-02-17), by Google and the Google Books Ngram contributors.
https://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv3.html
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Changes: lowercased and summed word and word-pair counts for 1980–2019, filtered
spellings, selected informative pairs, and quantized probabilities into HXTM v3.
The English capitalization list (en.capitals) comes from the same corpus's
word counts for 1980–2019: the share of each word written with a capital first letter.
English literal validity also uses SCOWL words, proper names, uppercase forms and abbreviations
outside the frequency model, lowercased and sorted; these spellings carry no word probabilities.
English spellings: SCOWL 2020.12.07, sizes through 70; Kevin Atkinson and contributors.
Permission and individual source notices: scowl.txt. https://wordlist.aspell.net/

Swedish keyboard language model (sv.hextm), capitalization list (sv.capitals) and validity list
(sv.valid): word and word-pair counts from the Leipzig Corpora Collection (swe_news_2019_1M,
swe_news_2020_1M, swe_news_2022_1M, swe_news_2023_1M, swe_wikipedia_2016_1M, swe_wikipedia_2021_1M,
swe-se_web_2019_1M), Universität Leipzig, https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de,
and from the OpenSubtitles v2024 Swedish monolingual text (http://www.opensubtitles.org), as
distributed by OPUS (P. Lison and J. Tiedemann, 2016), https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php
Spelling filter: the LibreOffice sv_SE Hunspell dictionary by Göran Andersson, GNU Lesser General
Public License 3: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html
https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/master/sv_SE

left out words far commoner in English, selected informative pairs and quantized probabilities
into HXTM v3. Build script: android/benchmark/typing/build-swedish-model.py.
